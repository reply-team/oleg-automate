## What it costs

The bot runs in Claude Tag, Claude's own app for Slack, on Anthropic's servers — no server of your
own. It comes with Claude's Team plan: $20 per person a month billed yearly, $25 monthly
(claude.com/pricing). Answers in shared channels are billed by usage on top, with a monthly limit
you set. Claude Tag is in public beta. Slack: works on the Free plan ($0, up to 3 apps); Pro is
$8.75 per person a month, or $7.25 billed yearly (slack.com/pricing). The Google Drive and Google
Calendar connectors are made by Google; sign in with Google and press Allow once.

Prices checked 2026-09-23.
