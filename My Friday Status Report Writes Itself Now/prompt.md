# The prompt from the video

Paste this into a new chat in the Claude desktop app, with the Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive connectors
turned on. Change the lines marked `[change:]`.

```
Look through my Gmail, my Google Calendar and the project plan in Google Drive for this week. Write a status report as a new Google Doc under four headings: done, moved, at risk, next. Put a link to the email or document behind every line. Then tell me in the chat which lines you are not sure about.
Only use emails to or from the eight people on my team.
Keep the whole report to one page.
```

[change:] «the project plan» — the name of your plan document in Drive.
[change:] «the eight people on my team» — your team (or list their names).
