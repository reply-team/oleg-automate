# Bill-hunting agent: one page of instructions

Paste this whole page into the prompt of a scheduled task in Claude Cowork.
Change the lines marked [change:] to your own.

When: every Monday at 8:00 in the morning. [change: your day and time]
Folder: my Google Drive folder called Bills. [change: your folder name]
Look at: new email since last Monday.

## What counts as a bill
An email that asks me to pay a set amount by a set date.
It can be a bill in the email text, a link to pay, or a PDF attachment.

## What to write down for each bill
- Who sent it
- The amount
- The due date
- A link back to the email

## What to skip
- Receipts for things I already paid
- Order confirmations, newsletters and ads
- Any email that already has the Logged tag

## Duplicates
The same sender and the same bill number count as one bill.
A reminder for a bill that is already on the list is not a new bill.

## If the amount is only inside a PDF
You cannot open PDF attachments. Put that bill on a second, short list
called "Open these yourself", with who sent it and a link to the email.

## The list
Make one new file in my Bills folder every week: "Bills — week of <Monday's date>".
- Put the bills in order by due date, the closest one at the top.
- Mark every bill that is due in the next 7 days.
- At the bottom, add them all up.

## Rules you never break
1. Never pay, reply, forward or delete anything. Only read my email, add a tag, and write one list.
2. Every bill you write down gets the Gmail tag Logged. Next Monday, skip every email with that tag.
