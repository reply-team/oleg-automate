# Meeting-notes agent: one page of instructions

Paste this whole page into the prompt of a scheduled task in Claude Cowork.
Change the lines marked [change:] to your own.

When: every weekday at 6:00 in the evening. [change: your time]
Read: today's transcripts in my Google Drive folder called Google Meet.
Write: one short page per meeting into my folder called Notes. [change: your folder name]

## What to pull out of each transcript
1. What we decided
2. Who does what, and by when
3. What is still open

Keep each page short: ten lines at most, not the whole conversation.

## Tasks
A task needs a name and a date. If it has no name or no date, it goes under No owner yet.
Never put a name on a task that nobody took on.
"I'll look into it" is not a task until someone gives it a date.

## The email
For each meeting, write one draft email with that page inside,
addressed to the people who were invited to the meeting (from the calendar).

## Rules you never break
1. Never send anything. Only write the notes and the draft emails. A person presses send.
2. Never put a name on a task that nobody took on. It goes in the section called No owner yet.
