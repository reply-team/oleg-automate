# The source article

Everything in [`12-formats.md`](12-formats.md) is cut from one real, published article:

**"The Ultimate 2026 Guide to ChatGPT/AI Cold Emails"**
<https://reply.io/blog/chatgpt-ai-cold-emails/>
Eugene Suslov · Reply.io blog · published 14 Sep 2025

The full text is not reproduced here — it is the author's, it lives at the link, and a copy in this
folder would go stale the first time it is edited. What follows is a map: enough to check any of the
twelve pieces against the thing it came from without re-reading fourteen minutes of article.

## What it argues

Cold email is not spam — spam is lazy and irrelevant, cold email is targeted outreach to someone who
could actually benefit. AI has made cold email smarter rather than spammier, but it has also made it
easy to get lazy: plug in a prompt, hit generate, hope. The guide's position is that AI should help
your thinking rather than replace it, and most of the article is the specific discipline that keeps
that true.

## The lines the twelve pieces are built on

These are the load-bearing claims. Where a piece in `12-formats.md` quotes or paraphrases, it is
one of these.

- **Subject line length.** "The best-performing subject lines are 1–5 words long. Emails with 1–3
  word subjects had higher reply rates than longer ones." The section's own rule of thumb is "keep it
  short (ideally 3–4 words)"; the closing checklist states "3–5 words, clean and personal."
- **The first line.** "The first line matters almost as much as the subject line. This is where you
  show the email was written **for them, not at them**."
- **Clickbait.** Fake-familiar subjects such as "Re: our meeting" when there was no meeting are named
  as a thing to avoid.
- **Length.** Under 70 words, 2–3 short paragraphs, fifteen seconds to read. The value section alone
  is "1–2 short sentences, 54 words total."
- **The structure.** Subject line → intro/hook → reason for outreach → value with proof → one CTA →
  optional P.S. → plain signature.
- **The anti-robot check.** Did you tweak the output or copy-paste it? Did you research the person
  first? Would you reply to this yourself?
- **AI and the human touch.** The article's closing position: AI assists, it does not replace — the
  future it describes is "AI SDRs + human touch," not AI alone.
