## The source article

Every piece in `12-formats.md` is cut from one real, published article:

**"The Ultimate 2026 Guide to ChatGPT/AI Cold Emails"** — <https://reply.io/blog/chatgpt-ai-cold-emails/>

It is the article on screen in the video. Its full text is not copied into this folder; `example-post.md`
maps it so any of the twelve pieces can be checked against the thing it came from.

## What to change

The video's agent is told to make **4 LinkedIn, 5 X, 1 newsletter, 2 threads** — twelve pieces. That
mix is the one thing you have to edit: it is this channel's mix, not yours. The rules, the reject-ifs
and the shape of each piece work unchanged wherever you publish.

The second thing worth copying deliberately is the review queue. Twelve pieces landed at 7:00 AM and
four came back edited. The system is built so nothing reaches an audience without a person having
looked at it, and that is the step to protect when someone suggests automating the last mile.
