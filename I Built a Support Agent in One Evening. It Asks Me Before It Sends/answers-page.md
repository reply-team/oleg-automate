# The page of answers

The questions people ask most, each with the answer we always give.
Swap these for your own: copy them from replies you have already sent.
Lines marked [change:] are the ones every shop has to fill in.

## Where's my order?
Your order [order number] from [date] is on its way. Here is the tracking link: [tracking link].

## When will my order arrive?
We ship in [change: your shipping time]. Your order [order number] from [date] left on [ship date].

## Can I change the address?
Yes, if the order has not shipped yet. Reply with the new address and we will update order [order number].
If it has already shipped, we will tell you how to get it redirected.

## Can I change what I ordered?
Yes, if the order has not shipped yet. Reply with what you want instead and we will update order [order number].

## Did you get my payment?
Yes, order [order number] from [date] is paid. You will get an email when it ships.
