The two files from the video:

📄 Rules file — <LINK>
📄 Report template — <LINK>

The rules file is what turns a general-purpose coding agent into a weekly status writer. It tells
it what to read (your tracker for what moved, your chat for decisions and blockers, your two or
three weekly numbers), what to write, and — the part that actually matters — what it is not
allowed to do:

• it can't soften "blocked" into "almost done"
• it can't promote "in progress" to "done"
• anything untouched for 7+ days goes in Blocked with the day count, whether or not anyone called
  it blocked

That last rule is why the reports got more honest. A person writing their own status has a reason
not to mention the task they haven't touched. An agent doesn't.

Works with whatever tracker and chat you already use — nothing in either file names a product.

Two honest limits, both in the rules file: the agent only reports what your tools can see, so
decisions made in calls and never written down won't show up. And keep the human review — once a
quarter something needs framing that raw status can't give.

How long does your weekly report take? Multiply that by fifty-two before you answer what you'd do
with the time.
